Game Rules

Classic

After each guess, every characteristic is compared with the correct agent.

Correct

The characteristic matches.

Incorrect

The characteristic does not match.

Partial

The characteristic partially matches.

Race categories

Agents are divided into four race categories.

Others includes rare races that do not belong to the three main categories.

Endless

Guess as many agents as possible without a daily limit. Characteristics are compared in the same way as in Classic mode.

5 tries

You have five tries to guess each agent.

Score
12

The score shows how many agents you have guessed.

Pixel

Guess the agent from their pixelated silhouette.

Splash Art

Guess the agent from a fragment of their splash art.

Bangboo

Guess the hidden Bangboo using the available clues.

The first clue is available at the beginning of the round. Each incorrect guess reveals the next clue.
